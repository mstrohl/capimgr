# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in capimgr to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive
for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and collaborative.
- Assume good intent while discussing disagreements about technical decisions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption,
and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to the project maintainers
through GitHub. Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. Maintainers
will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical, and may
remove content or restrict participation when necessary. Maintainers are expected
to apply this policy fairly and consistently.

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and
project-related discussions.
